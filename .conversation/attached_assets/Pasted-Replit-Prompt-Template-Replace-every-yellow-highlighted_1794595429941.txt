Project request
Build a professional, responsive, multi-page academic personal website for me using the materials I uploaded to this Replit project.
My information
Name: [FULL NAME]
Current position and institution: [POSITION OR PROGRAM AND INSTITUTION]
Website structure
Create a multi-page website with separate pages for:
1. About
2. Research
3. CV
4. Contact
Additional pages: [TYPE NONE OR LIST ADDITIONAL PAGES]
Use the About page as the main homepage.
Include a consistent navigation menu on every page. The website name or logo in the navigation bar should return visitors to the About page.
About page
Use my uploaded profile image as the main professional photograph.
Use about-information.docx to create the page content. The page should include:
• My name and current position
• My institutional affiliation
• A short professional biography
• My primary research areas
• A concise introduction to my research program
• Links to my professional profiles
• A button that directs visitors to the Research page
• A button to download my CV
Present my research interests in an attractive, easy-to-scan format. My main research areas include [LIST YOUR MAIN RESEARCH AREAS].
Research page
Create a rich Research page rather than displaying only a short list of publications.
Begin with a brief overview explaining that [WRITE ONE SENTENCE DESCRIBING THE OVERALL QUESTION YOUR RESEARCH ADDRESSES]. Organize my research into clear themes based on the information in my CV and About document.
Create a prominent Featured Study section using featured-study.pdf.
The featured study is [FEATURED STUDY TITLE]. Present it as an accessible research story rather than simply reproducing the paper abstract.
Before writing the Featured Study section, read featured-study.pdf and extract:
• The study title
• The research purpose or central question
• The study design, method, and sample
• The major findings
• Why the study matters
Use only information supported by the uploaded paper. Do not invent or change findings, statistics, sample sizes, methods, or citations. If anything is unclear, use a clearly labeled placeholder or ask me before adding it.
The Featured Study section should include:
• Study title
• Brief overview
• Central research question
• Why the topic matters
• Study design and participants
• Major findings
• Theoretical and practical implications
• A short takeaway for a general academic audience
• A link to the paper or publication, if available
Use featured-figure.png in this section. Display the correct original caption beneath it and provide a short, plain-language explanation of what the figure shows.
Do not alter the figure or invent a new caption. If the correct caption is not clear from the uploaded materials, ask me. Make sure the figure remains readable on desktop and mobile screens.
Below the Featured Study section, create a Selected Publications section using information from my CV. Organize publications by topic or research area when appropriate. Do not limit the page to only three publications if the CV provides additional relevant work.
Only include publication information found in my uploaded materials. Do not invent titles, authors, journal names, dates, findings, statistics, or links.
After building the Research page, show me a short verification list containing the study title, method and sample, key findings, and figure caption so I can compare them with the original paper.
CV page
Use cv.pdf.
Create:
• A short introduction
• An embedded CV viewer if it works reliably
• A clearly visible Download CV button
• A backup link that opens the PDF in a new browser tab
Do not manually rewrite the entire CV on this page.
Contact page
Use the contact information and professional links provided in about-information.docx.
Include:
• Institutional email address
• Department and university affiliation
• Google Scholar
• ORCID
• LinkedIn
• ResearchGate, lab website, or other professional link, if provided
Make email addresses and professional links clickable.
Do not create a working contact form that requires a server or database. A simple email button is sufficient for this first version.
Design instructions
Use a clean, professional academic design inspired by [UNIVERSITY, WEBSITE, OR STYLE YOU WANT TO USE AS INSPIRATION].
My preferred colors are: [LIST YOUR COLORS OR ASK AGENT TO CHOOSE COLORS BASED ON YOUR DESIGN REFERENCE]
My design reference is: [PASTE A WEBSITE LINK OR DESCRIBE THE VISUAL STYLE OR SCREENSHOT]
The website should feel [CHOOSE THREE TO SIX WORDS SUCH AS PROFESSIONAL, ACADEMIC, MODERN, MINIMAL, WELCOMING, CREATIVE, OR ACCESSIBLE].
Use the design reference for general inspiration, but do not copy another website exactly or imply that the website is an official university website.
Use:
• Generous white space
• Clear page headings
• Readable typography
• Subtle section dividers
• Professional cards or panels where appropriate
• Large, readable research figures
• Minimal and purposeful visual effects
Avoid excessive animations, decorative graphics, gradients, or a corporate marketing style.
